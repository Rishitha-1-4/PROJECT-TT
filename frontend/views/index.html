Critical Urgency Sample Advisory
Brake Noise & Grinding Pedal

Symptoms: High-pitched squeal or metal-on-metal grinding when applying brake pedal.

Potential Causes: Worn brake pads past minimum thickness indicator, damaged brake rotors.

Safety Warning: Severely compromised braking performance. Stop driving if pedal sinks or loses resistance.
